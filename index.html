Progression rules

Use double progression. Reach the top of the rep range on every prescribed set at the target RIR before increasing load. Only increase when technique is sound.

Bodyweight / jump / sleep

Training principles

  • RIR = reps in reserve; 2 RIR means about two clean reps left.
  • Warm-up sets do not count as working sets. Use controlled reps.
  • Compound lifts: 2–3 minutes rest; isolation: 60–90 seconds; jumps: recover fully.
  • Most working sets finish at 1–2 RIR. Do not grind reps.
  • Stop if sharp pain occurs. Normal effort is different from joint pain.

Weekly plan

Monday: Upper A · Tuesday: Volleyball · Wednesday: Upper B · Thursday: Lower + abs · Friday: Upper C · Saturday: Optional tune-up · Sunday: Basketball.

If fatigue accumulates, skip the optional session. If performance or recovery deteriorates, reduce working sets for a week.
